v3.6 - Major Update bringing new features and fixes

- ZIM dictionaries support - get yours at https://library.kiwix.org/
- morphology: searching 'understood' shows results for 'understand', built-in for English
- morophology for other languages: click ☰ > ⚙️ > click *Lemmatization*
- DSL attachments and video support
- wudict desktop only: download morphology languages from console: `wudict lemmas list` then e.g. `wudict lemmas download es fr de it`
- wudict desktop only: `wudict dump -o somedir /path/dict.mdx` to export to a pyglossary-compatible .CSV
