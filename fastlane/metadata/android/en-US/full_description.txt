wuDict searches all dictionary files on your own phone, with no
account and no network.

The app runs the wudict server locally and shows its pages in a WebView. Your
dictionaries stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked,
there is no sign-in — the app works fully offline.

SUPPORTED FORMATS

- MDict (.mdx/.mdd)
- StarDict (.ifo/.idx/.dict)
- Aard 2 (.slob)
- Lingvo DSL (.dsl)
- Babylon (.bgl)

Pictures, audio pronunciation and the dictionary's own styling all render the
way the dictionary authors intended.

SEARCH

Four ways to look a word up:

- Exact — the headword itself
- Prefix — everything starting with what you typed
- Contains — the word anywhere in a headword
- Full text — the word anywhere in an article

Results from all of your dictionaries arrive together, streamed as each one
answers, so a large collection stays responsive.

BUILT FOR LARGE COLLECTIONS

Dictionaries are searched directly as soon as you add them. In the background
each one is prepared into a compact local index, after which lookups are
effectively instant even across a hundred dictionaries. Preparation is
automatic and needs no configuration; contains-search, full-text search and
media packing are per-dictionary switches you can turn on only for the dictionaries
that you select.

PRIVACY

- No account, no cloud, no telemetry
- No advertising
- The only network permission is used to reach the server running inside the
  app itself, on 127.0.0.1
- This build declares no storage permission at all: dictionaries are imported
  through the system file picker into the app's own storage
